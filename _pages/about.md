    <h2 id="about-heading">About</h2>
    <div class="profile-narrative">
      <p>Ben is pursuing a bachelor's degree in computer science at the University of New Mexico, with an expected graduation date of May 2027. He plans to begin a master's degree in computer science the following fall to deepen his understanding of the mathematics and algorithms underlying machine learning, including how models are designed and built from first principles.</p>

      <p>Since early 2024, Ben has worked as a year-round technical intern at Sandia National Laboratories, supporting and contributing to applications developed by his software team. His experience includes web application development, software testing, data warehousing, and data reporting. With guidance from senior developers, he designed and implemented pipelines that use large language models to help migrate C# test suites between mocking frameworks. He has also gained experience with code reviews, pull requests, Agile practices, and iterating on feedback from senior engineers. His core technologies include C#, Java, Python, Microsoft SQL Server, unit testing, and mocking.</p>

      <p>Outside of computing, Ben enjoys spending time outdoors, working out, reading, and learning new hobbies&mdash;most recently, golf. He welcomes conversations with faculty, graduate programs, and engineering teams working on dependable software or the practical integration of machine learning into larger systems.</p>
